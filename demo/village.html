Village builder (demo)

Pick where to found your town. Hover the map: the three hexes that meet at the corner nearest the pointer light up, green if you can settle them (open land on one level), red if not. The white dot is where the well and square go. Click to found it.

Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.

Tap a hex to build there: Lay cobblestone takes it into the town (it has to join it); then any building that fits can go on the cobbles, even across hexes on one level. Road goes a hex at a time, as in the game.

Buildings never move once built. A building fits if it overlaps nothing, hides no more than a third of the one behind it (nor its door), and every door in town can still be walked to from the well. "Grow it" builds for you, laying cobblestone a hex at a time when the town is full; two-storey town houses go up once it has 100 people.
